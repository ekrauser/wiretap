Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - Factorio 2.1 support. Electric networks are read through the 2.1 LuaElectricNetwork API: a
      network joined across closed power switches (or a platform's global network) is reported once,
      keyed by its lowest sub network id, and carries a sub_network_count. Single-group networks keep
      the key they had in 1.0.x.
    - Per-network "flow" section (setting "Export power flow and satisfaction", on by default):
      consumption_satisfaction (1 = no brownout), production_satisfaction, maximum_production_watts,
      maximum_consumption_watts, solar_watts, transfer_watts. Surface power totals gain
      consumption_satisfaction (demand-weighted), maximum_production_watts, maximum_consumption_watts
      and solar_watts.
    - Space platforms per force (setting "Export space platforms", on by default): state, speed,
      location, last_location, distance, weight, completed_trips, has_hub and deletion_in_ticks,
      keyed by the platform's surface name so it joins with the matching surfaces entry.
    - Production totals broken down by quality (setting "Break down production totals by quality",
      off by default): items_produced_total_by_quality and items_consumed_total_by_quality.
    - Surfaces report planet and pollutant (pollution or spores); meta reports game_version; research
      reports current_units (research units the current technology needs).
  Changes:
    - Requires Factorio 2.1. Factorio 2.0 games stay on wiretap 1.0.x.
    - The snapshot is encoded by the mod instead of helpers.table_to_json, which prints 30+ digit
      expansions for most fractional numbers. Integers print as integers, fractions with the configured
      number of decimals (trailing zeros trimmed), object keys are sorted, non-finite numbers become
      null. The "Number precision" setting now actually shrinks the file.
    - Accumulator charge and capacity come from the electric network's last-tick flow instead of a scan
      of every accumulator entity. The "Scan accumulators" setting is gone; charge and capacity are
      always part of the power section.
    - The export timer is only re-registered when the interval setting changes.

Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Factorio 2.1 support. Electric networks are read through the 2.1 LuaElectricNetwork API: a
      network joined across closed power switches is reported once, keyed by its lowest sub network id,
      and carries a sub_network_count. Space platform grids keep the key "global".
    - Per-network "flow" section (setting "Export power flow and satisfaction", on by default):
      consumption_satisfaction (1 = no brownout) plus consumption_satisfaction_min, the lowest value
      sampled once a second since the previous export, production_satisfaction, maximum production
      and consumption, solar offered and used, primary and secondary generation, accumulator charge
      and discharge watts, transferred watts. Surface totals gain the same aggregates.
    - Space platforms per force (setting "Export space platforms", on by default): state, paused,
      speed, location, destination, route, last_location, distance, weight, completed_trips,
      damaged_tiles, has_hub and deletion_in_ticks, keyed by the platform's surface name.
    - Research: progress_per_minute and eta_seconds from consecutive exports, current_units,
      current_unit_energy, current_ingredients, queue_details with the level of each queued entry,
      technologies_available and technologies_infinite, labs per surface, lab_speed_modifier,
      enabled, and the effective research rate (science_per_minute, science_per_minute_10m,
      science_total) from the game's hidden "science" item.
    - Kills and losses per force and surface, all time and per minute (setting "Export kills and
      losses", on by default).
    - Entity build and mine counts per force and surface (setting "Export entity build counts",
      off by default).
    - Production totals broken down by quality (setting "Break down production totals by quality",
      off by default).
    - Surfaces report planet, pollutant (pollution or spores), daytime, darkness and
      solar_multiplier. Enemy evolution gains an evolution_by split (pollution, time, spawners) and
      is no longer listed for platforms.
    - Meta reports game_version, game_speed, tick_paused, active mods, feature flags and per-player
      afk_seconds, online_seconds and surface.
    - Science packs are recognised through the lab inputs (used_by_labs) instead of the name
      pattern alone, so modded packs with other names are included.
    - The timer export is spread over consecutive ticks, one surface or force per tick, and written
      when the last one is done (setting "Spread the export over several ticks", on by default);
      meta.export_ticks reports the span. Commands and the remote interface still build in one go.
  Changes:
    - Requires Factorio 2.1.15 or newer. Factorio 2.0 games stay on wiretap 1.0.x.
    - The snapshot is encoded by the mod instead of helpers.table_to_json, which prints 30+ digit
      expansions for most fractional numbers. Integers print as integers, fractions with the configured
      number of decimals (trailing zeros trimmed; fractions below 1 keep at least three), object keys
      are sorted, non-finite numbers become null. The "Number precision" setting now actually shrinks
      the file; its minimum is 1.
    - Accumulator charge and capacity come from the electric network's last-tick flow instead of a scan
      of every accumulator entity. The "Scan accumulators" setting is gone; charge and capacity are
      always part of the power section. Accumulators out of range of every pole are no longer counted.
    - Networks are dropped only when nothing was ever attached to them; an idle grid with machines
      stays listed with zeros instead of flickering in and out.
    - /wiretap write and remote write_now report export failures instead of claiming success; a
      repeating failure is logged once instead of every interval.
    - game_time_seconds derives from ticks_played; filename settings trim whitespace; the export
      timer is re-registered only when the interval changes and heals itself if another mod changes
      the setting; the mod version in meta comes from the mod list.
    - Licensed under MIT (LICENSE file added); mod author set to the portal account name.

Version: 1.0.3
Date: 2026-07-19
  Features:
    - Space platform power now works: surfaces with a global electric network (Factorio 2.0.48+)
      are read via surface.global_electric_network_statistics and reported as network "global",
      with accumulator charge attached to it. Entity-based reads can never work there
      (electric_network_statistics is electric-pole-only), so the 1.0.2 hub/accumulator
      candidate scan was removed.
  Changes:
    - Science setting description no longer claims to gate research progress; research state is
      always exported.

Version: 1.0.2
Date: 2026-07-18
  Features:
    - New "Number precision" setting (default 3): exported numbers are rounded to that many
      decimal places instead of full float64 precision, which meaningfully shrinks the JSON.
    - Per-surface count_by_entity under power: how many entities of each type appear in the
      power breakdown, so watts per type can be read as utilization per machine.
    - On surfaces where no electric pole or hub yields statistics (space platforms), accumulators
      are tried as network representatives as a fallback.
  Changes:
    - Science pack entries now always contain produced_total and consumed_total (0 instead of
      an absent key), so consumers need no null checks.
    - Networks reachable only through accumulators get a network entry (zero flows, real
      charge/capacity), so power.totals and power.networks agree.
    - All-zero networks (isolated poles: no production, consumption or storage) are no longer
      listed; network_count counts the listed networks.

Version: 1.0.1
Date: 2026-07-18
  Bugfixes:
    - "Write on server only" made every export a silent no-op in single player (for_player=0 only
